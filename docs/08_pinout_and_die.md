# 8. Pinout, pin count and die size (SCL180)

## 8.1 Pin count

| Level | Count | Breakdown |
|---|---|---|
| **Core boundary** (`t1c_riscv_soc`, no I/O ring) | **46 signal ports** + VDD, VSS | clk, rst, gpio_in/out/oen [8]×3, uart rxd/txd/txen, spi sclk/mosi/miso/ss[4], boot-flash sclk/mosi/miso/cs_n, i2c scl/sda × (i, o, oen) |
| **Chip** (`chip_top`, with I/O ring) | **40 bonded pads** | 26 signal + 14 power/ground |
| ↳ signal pads | 26 | 5 inputs, 11 outputs, 10 bidirectional |
| ↳ core supply 1.8 V | 8 | 4 VDD (`pvdi`) + 4 VSS (`pv0i`) — one pair per side |
| ↳ I/O supply 3.3 V | 6 | 3 VDDO (`pvda`) + 3 VSSO (`pv0a`) |
| Corner cells | 4 | `pfrelr`, physical only (no bond pad) |

Why 46 → 26: each bidirectional pad merges three core ports (data out,
output enable, data in): GPIO 24 → 8, I2C 6 → 2.

Power-pad count from SCL's `an_tsl18cio250sso.pdf`: a core supply pad
carries ≤ 70 mA on a 4-metal stack and every I/O must be within 9 pad slots
of a PVDI (ESD); the core draws ≈ 280 mW / 1.62 V ≈ 170 mA at the SS corner
(SRAM power under-reported by the macro library) → 4 VDD + 4 VSS. VDDO/VSSO
pairs sit within 8 slots of each group of simultaneously switching outputs
(GPIO, SPI + flash, UART + I2C).

## 8.4 Die size

| Item | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| SRAM macros | 8 × 1797.22 × 879.72 µm = **12.65 mm²** | `rd3_4096x32.lef` |
| Standard-cell logic | **2.63 mm²** placed cell area (79 507 cells) | Innovus `check_place` |
| Macro block | 2 cols × 4 rows + 20 µm halos ≈ 3.65 × 3.62 mm | floorplan |
| Core | **4.4 × 4.4 mm = 19.4 mm²** | logic density 45 % in ~750 µm channels |
| Pad ring | 250 µm pads + 150 µm core-ring/routing gap per side | CIO250 LEF |
| **Die** | **5.2 × 5.2 mm = 27.0 mm²** | core + 2 × 400 µm |
| Without I/O ring | core box 4.4 × 4.4 mm (19.4 mm²) | |
| Pad pitch check | 10 pads/side × 65 µm = 0.65 mm of 4.7 mm available | core-limited, not pad-limited |

Why 5.2 mm and not 5.0 mm: a 5.0 mm die (4.2 mm core) was tried first. It
placed at 64 % density but left only ~550 µm logic channels beside the macro
block: early global-route overflow was 8.6 % (M3 18.5 %). At 5.2 mm the
overflow drops to 2.5 % (M3 4.7 %) and the worst congestion hotspot from
1019 to 45 (normalised) — see `pd/reports/` and `pd/reports_5000/`.

The die is set by the SRAM (65 % of the core). Halving the memory
(32 KB + 32 KB) would bring the die to ≈ 4 × 4 mm.

## 8.5 Package

40 bonded pads. Any package with ≥ 40 leads and a cavity/die-attach area
≥ 5.7 × 5.7 mm fits, e.g. CLCC-44 / CQFP-44 (11 per side; the 4 extra leads
can be bonded as additional VSS/VSSO). Confirm the bond diagram with the
SCL packaging options.
